👩‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

1. Boil the pasta in salted water until it is cooked.

2. Heat butter or oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden.

3. Add chopped onion and garlic and cook until soft.

4. Add cooking cream and milk. Mix well.

5. Add grated cheese and stir until the sauce becomes creamy.

6. Add the boiled pasta and mix everything together.

7. Cook for 2–3 minutes and serve hot. 🍝

🍝Chicken pasta

Dinner

🍝 Chicken Pasta Ingredients

-250g pasta
-250g boneless chicken
-1 medium onion, chopped
-2–3 cloves garlic, chopped
-1 cup cooking cream
-½ cup milk
-½ cup grated cheese
-1 tbsp butter or oil
